# Quizzuh

A music timeline quiz for the Lindén family, played on phones.

- A hub (computer or iPad) shows a QR code; everyone scans it with their phone to join.
- Each round a song plays. On your phone you type the artist and the song (and the year for a bonus),
  then drag your card into the right place on your own timeline.
- Songs come from the Spotify playlist "Quizzzuhhh". Each game picks a random set with no two songs from the same year.

Status: first playable version (hub + phones + scoring). Music needs a Spotify Client ID in `config.js`.
The checked song list is in `data/songs-checked.xlsx`.
